Your five-week plan

Two focused hours a day, built around one main skill and one supporting skill. The language banks on this page feed every session: pull ten new items into the day's writing and speaking, and recycle yesterday's ten before you start.

60 minMain skill of the day — full task practice
40 minSecond skill + corrections from yesterday
20 minSpeaking recording — listen back, note errors
10 phrasesNew items from the banks below, used aloud
Week 1 · Nov 7–14

Diagnostic + templates

Take Diagnostic Test 1, learn the exam pattern cold, and lock in response templates for every Writing and Speaking task type.

Weeks 2–3 · Nov 15–28

Skill rotation

Rotate the four skills daily. Build range: topic vocabulary, collocations and complex sentences in every written and spoken answer.

Week 4 · Nov 29–Dec 5

Timed sections

Every practice under real exam timing. Finish with Full Mock 1 on Dec 5 and a written error log of every mistake type.

Week 5 · Dec 6–12

Mocks + taper

Full Mocks 2 and 3 early in the week, light review after, exam on Dec 12. No new material in the final 48 hours — recycle and rest.

What CLB 10 actually demands

SkillFormatWhat a 10 looks like
Listening~47–55 min · 6 parts, everyday + workplace scenariosCatching implied meaning, attitude and detail in fast, natural speech — not just the main idea.
Reading~55–60 min · 4 parts, correspondence to viewpointsReading between the lines: tone, purpose and opinion, handled at speed without re-reading everything.
Writing53–60 min · email (150–200 words) + survey response (150–200 words)Clear organisation, varied sentence structures, precise vocabulary, correct register — and almost no errors.
Speaking15–20 min · 8 tasks, recorded responsesFluent, well-developed answers with natural collocations, a range of tenses and conditionals, and ideas extended with reasons and examples.
How to use this page: don't read the banks like a novel. Pick a topic, learn eight to ten entries properly — say each example aloud, then write your own sentence — and drop them into that day's practice task. Ten items a day, recycled, beats a hundred skimmed once.

Grammar core

Band 10 is not about exotic grammar — it is about using a wide range accurately. Master these five areas and your Writing and Speaking stop leaking marks: modal verbs, the twelve tenses, conditionals, punctuation, and complex sentences.
